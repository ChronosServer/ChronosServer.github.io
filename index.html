What is Chronos

Chronos is a technical Minecraft server, dedicated to making the best tech possible.

We run a network of worlds on our own high-performance hardware: a main survival SMP, creative worlds for building and testing, and snapshot servers for what comes next.

The hardware is an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D with 128 GB of DDR5 ECC memory, running every world in the network.

Besides the main SMP there are several creative worlds, a mirror world, snapshot servers for upcoming versions, and building, tour and public worlds.

Patrons also get the Chronos Season SMP, which resets every six months with a new gameplay style chosen by vote.
